Help with Binder

Binder is a question bank for the Life & Health insurance license exam. Most questions about the app are answered below.

I bought the full bank and it is locked

Open Settings (the gear on the Today tab) and choose "Restore a purchase". Make sure the iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase.

Does Binder cover my state?

Binder covers the general content that state Life & Health exams share. It does not yet include state-specific law. Your state's candidate handbook lists that part.

I think an answer is wrong

Write to us with the question's first few words and what you believe is right. Every report is checked against the law or the regulator's own publication, and corrections ship in the next update.

How do I start over?

Settings, then "Erase my progress". Your purchase is not affected.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Contact

Send us a message. We answer by email, usually within two business days.
