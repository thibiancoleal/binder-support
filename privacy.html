Binder

Privacy policy

In effect from November 26, 2026

Binder is a study app for the Life & Health insurance license exam. This page says what the app does with information about you. The short version: it does not collect any.

What Binder stores

Binder saves your study progress on your iPhone: which questions you answered, whether you got them right, when each one is due for review, the results of your sessions and the exam date you set. This file never leaves your device through Binder. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple includes it in that backup under Apple's own terms.

What Binder does not do

  • It has no accounts and asks for no name, email address or phone number.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or advertising software.
  • It does not send your answers or your progress to us or to anyone else.

Purchases

The full question bank is sold as an in-app purchase. Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or your Apple Account details. Apple tells the app only whether the purchase exists, so the app can unlock the content.

If you contact support

The support form is separate from the app and is hosted by Google Forms. If you write to us there, we receive what you type, including the email address you give, and we use it only to answer you.

Children

Binder is written for adults preparing for a professional license exam. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Erasing your data

Open Settings inside Binder and choose "Erase my progress", or delete the app. Either one removes everything Binder stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
